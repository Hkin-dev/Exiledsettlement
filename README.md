# Exiled Settlement 放逐聚落

Build and manage a survival settlement in Minecraft. Residents gather supplies, carry materials, construct buildings, and work in production facilities in the game world.

在 Minecraft 世界里建立和经营聚落，让居民实际领取物资、搬运材料、建造房屋，并参与生产。

**Early public test version. Back up your world before installing or updating.**

The mod is still in early development. Major changes to its underlying design and save format are likely, and compatibility with existing worlds is not guaranteed. Future updates may require starting a new world.

We recommend using a separate test world and avoiding a large investment of time or effort in a long-term save at this stage. If you want to use the mod for a stable, long-term playthrough, please wait until the full release before considering it.

**早期公开测试版，安装或更新前请备份存档。**

模组目前仍处于早期开发阶段，后续很可能对底层设计与存档结构进行较大调整，无法保证与旧存档兼容，更新时可能需要重新开档。

建议使用独立的测试存档体验，不建议在早期版本中投入过多时间和精力建设长期存档。如果希望将本模组用于稳定、长期的游玩，请至少等到正式版发布后再考虑。

## Design Philosophy · 设计理念

### English

1. **A simple, welcoming experience**

   I want Exiled Settlement to be a relaxing mod where players can build at their own pace, watch their residents go about their lives, and enjoy the settlement's gradual growth.

   I understand how satisfying it can be to overcome a difficult challenge, and I plan to add optional content for players who enjoy that. This is a lower priority, though, and may come much later in development. For now, the focus is on making the basic experience simple and welcoming.

2. **Less micromanagement**

   Constantly handling small details can demand more attention than planning the settlement as a whole. I want the mod to take care of those everyday chores, with residents managing the details of their work and reducing the need for repeated checks, commands, and intervention.

   Players should be free to focus on what they enjoy: planning the settlement, building and decorating, exploring the world, or simply watching what their residents are doing today. The mod should handle the routine details so players can enjoy the experience.

3. **As much customization as possible**

   Everyone has different preferences. I want to give players as much control over the mod's content as possible, allowing them to choose features and adjust values and progression speed to suit their own play style.

   The mod should offer a simple, welcoming default experience, with configuration providing additional choices. Players who want to jump straight in should not need to study the settings, while those who want to customize their experience should have room to do so.

### 中文

1. **简单、友好的游戏体验**

   我希望《放逐聚落》是一款轻松、休闲的模组，让玩家按照自己的节奏建设聚落、观察居民生活，享受逐渐发展的过程。

   我理解克服困难能够带来很大的满足感，也会在后续为喜欢挑战的玩家设计一些可选内容。不过，这部分的开发优先级会比较靠后，可能安排在开发的较后阶段；现阶段会优先打磨简单、友好的基础体验。

2. **非精细化管理**

   相比整体规划，反复处理细枝末节的事情往往更耗费心神。我希望把这些日常琐事交给程序，让居民能够自行处理工作中的具体安排，减少玩家反复检查、操作和干预的负担。

   玩家可以把注意力放在自己感兴趣的事情上：规划聚落、建造装饰、探索世界，或者只是看看居民今天在做什么。日常运转的细节由程序负责，玩家安心体验就好。

3. **尽可能开放自定义配置**

   每个人的喜好都不相同。我希望尽可能把模组内容的调整权交给玩家，让大家能够按照自己的偏好选择功能、调整数值和发展节奏，创造适合自己的游玩体验。

   模组会以简单、友好的默认体验为基础，自定义配置则提供更多选择。喜欢直接开始游戏的玩家无需研究设置，希望进一步调整的玩家也能有足够的空间。

## Future Plans · 未来计划

### English

Exiled Settlement will focus on relaxed settlement building and resident life by default, with ongoing work in the following areas:

1. **Improve the UI and information display**

   Refine layouts and controls so resident status, work progress, inventories, and resource needs are easier to find and understand.

2. **Improve the resident profession system**

   Refine each profession's responsibilities, workflows, and cooperation so residents can carry out their daily work more reliably with less repeated player intervention.

3. **Improve the overall play experience**

   Continue improving the introduction to the mod, interaction feedback, and progression pace to make founding, managing, and expanding a settlement smoother.

4. **Broaden mod compatibility**

   Aim to support as many commonly used open-source mods as possible, gradually testing them together and addressing conflicts as they are found.

5. **Refine values and balance**

   Adjust production, consumption, construction costs, and progression speed to make the default experience relaxing and rewarding, while leaving room for optional challenges.

6. **Improve algorithms and performance**

   Refine resident pathfinding, task assignment, and logistics scheduling to reduce unnecessary actions and stuttering, improving the experience at different settlement sizes.

7. **Explore integrations with common mods**

   Explore resource, recipe, and feature integrations with common mods whose gameplay fits the project, adding more ways to develop a settlement.

8. **Offer optional difficulty modes and settings**

   Keep the default experience relaxing while allowing players to choose difficulty modes or configure individual rules and pressures. Dedicated challenge content is a lower-priority, longer-term plan.

These are ongoing development directions. Specific content and priorities will be adjusted based on play feedback; there is no fixed release schedule for these plans.

### 中文

《放逐聚落》将以轻松建设和居民生活为默认体验，持续完善以下内容：

1. **完善 UI 与信息展示**

   优化界面布局和操作方式，让居民状态、工作进度、库存与资源需求更容易查看和理解。

2. **完善居民职业系统**

   梳理各职业的职责、工作流程与相互配合，让居民更可靠地完成日常工作，减少玩家反复干预。

3. **完善整体游玩体验**

   持续改善开局引导、操作反馈和发展节奏，让建立、经营和扩展聚落的过程更加顺畅。

4. **扩大模组兼容范围**

   尽可能兼容常见的开源模组，逐步验证共同运行时的表现，并处理发现的冲突。

5. **打磨数值与平衡**

   持续调整生产、消耗、建造成本和发展速度，使默认体验轻松、有成长感，同时为挑战玩法保留空间。

6. **优化算法与运行效率**

   改进居民寻路、任务分配、物流调度等算法，减少无效行动和卡顿，提升不同聚落规模下的游戏体验。

7. **探索常见模组联动**

   根据玩法契合度，与一些常见模组进行资源、配方或功能上的联动，丰富聚落的发展方式。

8. **提供可选难度与配置**

   保持默认体验轻松，让希望接受挑战的玩家自行选择难度模式，或通过配置调整各项规则与压力。专门的挑战内容属于优先级较低的长期计划。

以上为持续开发方向，具体内容和顺序会根据实际游玩反馈调整，暂不承诺各项计划的上线日期。

## Installation and first steps

### English

1. Install Minecraft 1.21.1 with the required NeoForge version and Java 21.
2. Download the mod `.jar` from [Releases](https://github.com/HkinXu/Exiledsettlement/releases) and place it in the instance's `mods` folder. Remove any other active version of this mod.
3. Restart Minecraft and start a new world for your first test.
4. Eligible players receive a starting camp blueprint on entering the world. Use it on the ground, choose a location, and confirm the camp.
5. Open the settlement core to manage residents and plan construction. Initial supplies are finite and need replenishment as the settlement grows.

For multiplayer, install the same mod version and compatible NeoForge on the server and participating clients.

### 中文

1. 安装 Minecraft 1.21.1、所需版本的 NeoForge 与 Java 21。
2. 从 [Releases 下载页面](https://github.com/HkinXu/Exiledsettlement/releases)下载模组 `.jar`，放入游戏实例的 `mods` 文件夹，移出本模组其他启用版本。
3. 完整重启游戏，首次体验建议使用新世界。
4. 尚未使用开局资格的玩家进入世界后会获得营地蓝图。手持蓝图右键地面，选择位置并确认营地。
5. 通过聚落核心管理居民和规划建设。初始库存有限，随着聚落发展需要继续补充物资。

联机时，服务端和参与的客户端均安装相同版本的本模组及兼容的 NeoForge。
